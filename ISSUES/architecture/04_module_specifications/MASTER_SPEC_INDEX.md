## Completion Gate Snapshot

Module completion is approved only when all are true:

1. Module score at least 85.
2. Diagram minimums and method minimums are satisfied.
3. Approval recorded in `ISSUES/architecture/01_contracts/APPROVAL_LOG.md`.
4. At least 4 core-heavy modules at 90 or above.
5. Portfolio average at or above 88.
